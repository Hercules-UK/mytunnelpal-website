Account & data

Delete your account

You can request deletion of your MyTunnelPal account and the personal data attached to it at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

How to request deletion

  • 1 Email support@mytunnelpal.comSend your request from the email address on your account, or include your MyTunnelPal username so we can find the right account.
  • 2 We confirm it's youWe may reply to verify the request before acting on it.
  • 3 Your account is restricted, then deletedYour account is restricted straight away, and your active personal data is deleted from our systems within 30 days.

Backup copies are deleted through our normal backup cycle shortly afterwards, and are never used as active account data in the meantime. A small number of records may be kept for longer where we have a lawful reason to retain them (for example, records we need to demonstrate compliance) — this is explained in full in our Privacy Policy.
