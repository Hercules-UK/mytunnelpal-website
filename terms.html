2. What MyTunnelPal is

MyTunnelPal is a free app for indoor skydivers (tunnel flyers). At its core, it lets you:

  • Log your flying sessions as a personal record.
  • Track your own skill progression on the constellation map.
  • Discover wind tunnels around the world.
  • Receive notifications you've chosen to receive.

3. What MyTunnelPal is not

This section matters more than any other, so it comes early.

MyTunnelPal is a record, not an instruction. The skills and sessions in the app are self-reported personal records. They are based entirely on what you tell the app about your own flying.

  • Marking a skill as achieved is not a certification. Nobody has verified it. It carries no official status anywhere.
  • Nothing in the app is instruction or coaching. Skill names and descriptions exist so you can identify and record what you've done, not so you can learn how to do it.
  • Nothing in the app gives you permission to attempt anything. Whether you're ready to try a skill is a decision for you and a qualified coach, made at the tunnel, not in an app.

Any skill order, level, badge, progress indicator, completion state or unlocked state is only a way to organise and motivate your personal record. It is not a training progression, a safety assessment, or a recommendation that you are ready to attempt a skill. Do not use it to decide what to attempt.

Tunnel flying skills are learned under the supervision of a qualified coach or instructor, following the rules and progression requirements of the tunnel you fly at. MyTunnelPal is never a substitute for that supervision. If you're under 18, this applies with even more force: what you attempt should be decided with the coach or instructor supervising you at the tunnel, and the app does not change that.
